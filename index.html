the last chapter · for the girl who makes ordinary things matter
🌹

For you,
Hilal.

There is a funny thing about some people: you don't always notice the exact moment they become important. There isn't necessarily a grand entrance or a dramatic piece of music. Sometimes they simply become part of the way you look at an ordinary day.

Maybe it's the way your optimism refuses to cooperate with pessimism. Maybe it's the absurd commitment you have to preparing for things long before everyone else has even remembered they exist. Maybe it's your wonderfully tuty fruity English, or the little things you forget five minutes after remembering them.

And perhaps it is the contrast of it all — the serious student, the ridiculous menace, the person who can make an entire meal from scratch, the girl who gets called a big baboon and somehow survives the insult with dignity.

I hope you never underestimate those little pieces of yourself. The things you think are ordinary may be exactly the things someone else remembers. A laugh. A strange phrase. A tiny act of kindness. A completely unnecessary argument. A conversation that should have lasted five minutes but somehow became an hour.

I don't know what every future chapter will call the people who walk through it. But I do know that knowing someone can be its own kind of gift, without needing to turn it into anything grander than what it naturally is.

So today, on 27 September, I hope you get to see yourself for a moment through the eyes of someone who thinks you are wonderfully, inconveniently, unmistakably you.

Happy birthday, my queen. Stay curious. Stay impossible. Keep that little spark. And please, for everyone's safety, never let the panda near your kitchen.

— your resident panda, who has somehow survived being called a panda ♡
